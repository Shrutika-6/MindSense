Technique: Self-Compassion Break
Emotion: sad, guilty, ashamed, disappointed, self-critical
Description: A mindfulness technique that replaces self-criticism with kindness toward yourself.
Steps:
- Place one hand on your heart and take a slow breath
- Say to yourself: "This is a moment of suffering"
- Say: "Suffering is a part of every human life — I am not alone"
- Say: "May I be kind to myself in this moment"
- Sit quietly for 60 seconds and breathe gently
When to use: When feeling guilty, ashamed, self-critical, or disappointed in yourself
Effectiveness: Reduces shame and self-criticism, builds emotional resilience over time
