Technique: 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding
Emotion: anxiety, panic, fear, dissociation, overwhelm
Description: A sensory awareness technique that anchors you to the present moment and interrupts anxiety spirals.
Steps:
- Name 5 things you can SEE around you
- Name 4 things you can physically TOUCH
- Name 3 things you can HEAR right now
- Name 2 things you can SMELL
- Name 1 thing you can TASTE
When to use: When feeling panicked, disconnected from reality, or overwhelmed
Effectiveness: Immediately interrupts anxiety by redirecting focus to present sensory experience
