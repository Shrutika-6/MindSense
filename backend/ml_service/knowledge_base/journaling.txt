Technique: Emotional Journaling
Emotion: sad, angry, confused, stressed, overwhelmed
Description: Writing thoughts and feelings freely to process emotions and gain clarity.
Steps:
- Find a quiet space and open a notebook or notes app
- Write without filtering — whatever comes to mind
- Describe what you are feeling and why
- Write what triggered this emotion
- End with one small thing you are grateful for today
When to use: When feeling emotionally heavy, confused, or overwhelmed
Effectiveness: Proven to reduce emotional intensity and improve mental clarity over time
