Technique: Positive Distraction
Emotion: sad, neutral, lonely, bored, low energy
Description: Intentionally engaging in a pleasant activity to shift emotional state when feeling low.
Steps:
- Identify one small activity you usually enjoy (music, walk, drawing, cooking)
- Commit to doing it for just 10 minutes — no pressure beyond that
- Focus fully on the activity, noticing small details and sensations
- After 10 minutes, check in with how you feel
When to use: When feeling sad, low energy, lonely, or emotionally flat
Effectiveness: Activates dopamine pathways and interrupts negative thought loops
